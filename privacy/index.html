TvGram Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

TvGram is an app for Android TV, Google TV and Android phones and tablets that shows the videos from your Telegram channels and chats as a TV feed. It is made by Meysam Shohrat (“we”). Contact: meysam.shohrat@gmail.com.

TvGram is an unofficial app built on the Telegram API. It is not made, endorsed or operated by Telegram.

In short
  • TvGram has no servers of its own. We never receive your messages, videos, watch history or account details.
  • Your data stays on your device, except what the app sends to Telegram to work, and purchases that Google Play handles.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don’t sell or share your data.

1. Data stored on your device

To work, TvGram keeps this in its private storage on your device. Other apps can’t read it, and it never leaves the device except as described in section 2:

  • Your Telegram session (sign-in), and the basic chat information Telegram’s own software keeps for it.
  • A catalog of video information from your chats: titles, captions, lengths, view and reaction counts, and thumbnails. Not the videos themselves.
  • What you watch and how far, so TvGram can resume videos and rank your feed. Recommendations are computed on the device.
  • Your lists (Watch later, Favorites, playlists), settings, and the profiles on the device, including kid profiles with their name and picture.
  • If you set a parental PIN: only a salted hash of it, never the PIN itself.
  • A small temporary cache of video while it plays. It’s deleted when you stop watching.

3. Children

TvGram is not directed at children under 13. Kid profiles are a parental control: an adult sets them up and picks the channels they show. TvGram collects no data from children; a kid profile’s history and lists are stored only on the device, like any other profile.

4. Security

Connections to Telegram are encrypted with Telegram’s MTProto protocol, and purchases go through Google Play over encrypted connections. Data on the device stays in the app’s private storage.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on the device stays until you remove it. Removing an account or profile in TvGram deletes its data from the device.
  • Uninstalling TvGram, or clearing its storage in the device’s settings, deletes everything TvGram stored.
  • Signing out also ends this device’s Telegram session. You can end it from your phone too: Telegram → Settings → Devices.
  • Your Telegram account and messages are kept by Telegram. To delete them, use Telegram.

6. Your rights

Because we don’t hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to give, correct or delete on a server. For data held by Telegram or Google, contact them through the links above. For any question about this policy, write to meysam.shohrat@gmail.com.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
