Delete your TvGram data

TvGram by Mesiiiii Masiiiii · Last updated: 12 October 2026

TvGram has no servers. Everything it stores is on your own device, so you can delete it yourself at any time. There is nothing to request from us, and nothing is kept by us afterwards.

Delete everything

Either of these deletes all TvGram data on the device straight away:

  • Uninstall TvGram.
  • Or open the device’s Settings → Apps → TvGram → Storage → Clear storage (on Android TV: Settings → Apps → TvGram → Clear data).

Delete one account or profile

  1. In TvGram, open the side menu and select your profile picture at the top.
  2. Select Manage accounts, then the account or kid profile, and confirm Remove.
  3. Its data is deleted from the device at once. For the account you are using, Settings → Sign out does the same.

What is deleted

  • Your Telegram sign-in session on this device (signing out also ends it on Telegram’s side).
  • The catalog of videos from your chats, thumbnails and the playback cache.
  • Watch history and progress, Watch later, Favorites, playlists and settings.
  • Kid profiles and, when you clear all data, the parental PIN.

There is no retention period: the data is removed immediately.

What is kept, and by whom

  • Your Telegram account and messages are kept by Telegram, not by TvGram. To end TvGram’s session from your phone, use Telegram → Settings → Devices. To delete your Telegram account, see Telegram’s FAQ.
  • Your TvGram Pro purchase is recorded by Google Play, so you can restore it after reinstalling. See the Google Privacy Policy.
